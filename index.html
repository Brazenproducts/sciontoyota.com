What Is Scion by Toyota?

On November 10, 2026, Toyota Motor North America introduced Scion by Toyota — a new brand built around curiosity, possibility, and the freedom to explore mobility in new ways.

Toyota calls it a "freedom lab": a space to challenge conventions and give drivers more room to experiment, personalize, and create something that's uniquely theirs. It builds on the experimental spirit of the original Scion brand, reimagined for a new generation. The three launch vehicles — KEI-01, SxS, and BKE — each represent a different possibility, from everyday utility to high-performance adventure to entirely new forms of personal mobility.

"The future of mobility will not be defined by one vehicle, one technology or one way of thinking. Scion by Toyota gives us a place to challenge conventional thinking, explore new possibilities and create mobility in new ways." — Simon Humphries, Chief Branding Officer & Head of Design, Toyota Motor Corporation

The KEI-01: Where the Journey Begins

The first production vehicle of the Scion by Toyota brand.

The KEI-01 is an ultra-compact utility truck that brings the functionality, efficiency, and versatility of Japan's legendary kei trucks to American customers — packaged the way only Toyota can deliver. Designed to meet low-speed vehicle (LSV) federal safety requirements, it's an affordable, highly capable, remarkably maneuverable little workhorse.

  • Engine: 660cc three-cylinder
  • Transmission: Continuously Variable Transmission (CVT)
  • Drivetrain: Four-wheel drive (4WD)
  • Classification: Low-Speed Vehicle (LSV)
  • Built: Toyota Motor Manufacturing Indiana — $34M investment, ~50 new jobs
  • Arrival: Second half of 2027
  • Initial sales: Fleet-focused pilot through select Scion by Toyota dealers (retail may follow later)

As an intentionally low-volume, niche vehicle, the KEI-01 will start with a fleet pilot program so Toyota can learn how it best serves U.S. customers and use cases. Based on demand, availability could expand to limited retail sales down the road.

SxS Concept — Beyond Limits

First shown at the 2025 SEMA Show, the SxS Concept reimagines the side-by-side for a new generation of outdoor enthusiasts. Developed by Toyota's North American engineering team, it combines a high-output turbocharged four-cylinder hybrid powertrain producing more than 300 horsepower with a compact, purpose-built off-road package.

Designed for everything from spirited desert running to technical rock crawling, it features significant suspension articulation, a nimble footprint, and a Silent Mode that lets it run on electric power alone — a whole new way to experience the outdoors. Toyota says it will keep advancing the concept and evaluating how it could bring an entirely new kind of off-road experience to enthusiasts.

  • Powertrain: Turbocharged 4-cylinder hybrid, 300+ hp
  • Silent Mode: electric-only operation
  • Built for: desert running to rock crawling
  • Debut: 2025 SEMA Show

BKE Concept — Beyond Permission

The BKE Concept explores another dimension of personal mobility — bringing together compact packaging, utility, and adventure. Where the KEI-01 emphasizes practical utility and the SxS showcases off-road performance, the BKE opens up the space in between: the freedom to choose your own direction, take the unexpected turn, and explore "beyond permission."

Its small, highly adaptable form creates new possibilities for different lifestyles, activities, and experiences — a glimpse at how Scion by Toyota is thinking about the future of how people move through and experience the outdoors.

Assembled in America

The KEI-01 will be assembled at Toyota Motor Manufacturing Indiana starting in the second half of 2027, supported by a $34 million investment that adds approximately 50 new jobs. It's part of Toyota's continued commitment to investing in the U.S. and American jobs.
