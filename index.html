What Is Scion by Toyota?

On November 10, 2026, Toyota Motor North America introduced Scion by Toyota — a new brand built around curiosity, possibility, and the freedom to explore mobility in new ways.

Toyota calls it a "freedom lab": a space to challenge conventions and give drivers more room to experiment, personalize, and create something that's uniquely theirs. It builds on the experimental spirit of the original Scion brand, reimagined for a new generation. The three launch vehicles — KEI-01, SxS, and BKE — each represent a different possibility, from everyday utility to high-performance adventure to entirely new forms of personal mobility.

"The future of mobility will not be defined by one vehicle, one technology or one way of thinking. Scion by Toyota gives us a place to challenge conventional thinking, explore new possibilities and create mobility in new ways." — Simon Humphries, Chief Branding Officer & Head of Design, Toyota Motor Corporation

Assembled in America

The KEI-01 will be assembled at Toyota Motor Manufacturing Indiana starting in the second half of 2027, supported by a $34 million investment that adds approximately 50 new jobs — part of Toyota's continued commitment to investing in U.S. manufacturing.
