Scion Is Back: Toyota Revives the Brand With the KEI-01, SxS & BKE

November 12, 2026 · Scion by Toyota News

It's official: Scion is back. On November 10, 2026, Toyota Motor North America introduced Scion by Toyota at simultaneous events in Ypsilanti Township, Michigan, and Frisco, Texas — reviving the beloved brand name as a "freedom lab" built around curiosity, possibility, and the freedom to explore mobility in new ways.

And this isn't a one-car revival. Toyota pulled the wraps off three distinct vehicles — the production-bound KEI-01, the SxS Concept, and the BKE Concept — each exploring a different idea of what mobility can be.

The KEI-01: A Kei Truck for America

The star is the KEI-01, an ultra-compact utility truck bringing Japan's legendary kei-truck formula to the U.S. It arrives in the second half of 2027 and comes standard with:

  • A 660cc three-cylinder engine
  • A continuously variable transmission (CVT)
  • Four-wheel drive (4WD)
  • Classification as a Low-Speed Vehicle (LSV) under federal safety requirements

It'll be assembled at Toyota Motor Manufacturing Indiana, backed by a $34 million investment adding roughly 50 jobs. Initially it's a fleet-focused pilot sold through a small network of select Scion by Toyota dealers — with possible limited retail sales down the road depending on demand.

SxS Concept: 300+ HP Hybrid Off-Roader

First shown at the 2025 SEMA Show, the SxS Concept reimagines the side-by-side with a turbocharged four-cylinder hybrid powertrain making more than 300 horsepower, significant suspension articulation, and a "Silent Mode" that lets it run on electric power alone. It's built for everything from desert running to technical rock crawling — and Toyota says it'll keep developing the concept.

BKE Concept: Personal Mobility, Reimagined

The BKE Concept explores the space between utility and off-road performance — a compact, highly adaptable concept focused on personal mobility and outdoor freedom. Toyota frames it as "beyond permission": the freedom to take the unexpected turn.

"The future of mobility will not be defined by one vehicle, one technology or one way of thinking. The KEI-01, SxS and BKE concepts each represent a different possibility — and together, they show what can happen when curiosity leads the way." — Simon Humphries, Chief Branding Officer & Head of Design, Toyota Motor Corporation
