Scion SxS Concept

A purpose-built hybrid side-by-side that reimagines the off-road vehicle — 300+ horsepower, Silent Mode electric drive, and a nimble, trail-ready footprint.

First shown at the 2025 SEMA Show, the SxS Concept reimagines the side-by-side for a new generation of outdoor enthusiasts. Developed by Toyota's North American engineering team, it represents Scion by Toyota's willingness to take Toyota technology and engineering into entirely new spaces.

Powertrain & Performance

At its heart is a high-output turbocharged four-cylinder hybrid powertrain producing more than 300 horsepower, paired with a compact, purpose-built off-road package. That's a serious step beyond typical side-by-side output — and the hybrid architecture adds a twist most off-roaders don't have.

  • Powertrain: Turbocharged 4-cylinder hybrid
  • Output: More than 300 horsepower
  • Silent Mode: electric-only operation for quiet trail running
  • Suspension: Significant articulation for technical terrain
  • Footprint: Compact and nimble
  • Developed by: Toyota's North American engineering team

Built for Everything

Designed for everything from spirited desert running to technical rock crawling, the SxS Concept features significant suspension articulation and a nimble footprint. Its Silent Mode lets it operate on electric power alone — offering a genuinely different, quieter way to experience the outdoors without spooking wildlife or shattering the calm.

Concept, Not Confirmed — Yet

The SxS Concept is more than an exercise in imagination. Toyota has said it will continue advancing the concept, exploring its potential, and evaluating how it could bring an entirely new kind of off-road experience to enthusiasts. It represents how Scion by Toyota wants to explore the intersection of performance, hybrid technology, and off-road adventure.
