Scion BKE Concept

A compact, highly adaptable concept exploring a new dimension of personal mobility — the space between utility and off-road performance.

The BKE Concept explores another dimension of personal mobility — bringing together compact packaging, utility, and adventure in a concept designed to expand how people move through and experience the outdoors.

The Space In Between

Where the KEI-01 emphasizes practical utility and the SxS Concept showcases off-road performance, the BKE Concept opens up the space in between — offering the freedom to choose your own direction, take the unexpected turn, and explore "beyond permission."

Its small, highly adaptable form creates new possibilities for different lifestyles, activities, and experiences. It's the most open-ended of the three launch vehicles — a glimpse at how Scion by Toyota is thinking about entirely new forms of getting around.

Part of a Bigger Vision

Together, the KEI-01, SxS, and BKE illustrate the breadth of the Scion by Toyota "laboratory" — from everyday utility, to high-performance adventure, to entirely new forms of personal mobility. The BKE represents the brand's willingness to push past conventional categories entirely.

As a concept, details remain limited and specifications have not been fully announced. We'll expand this page as Toyota shares more about the BKE's form, powertrain, and future.
