Scion KEI-01

The first production vehicle of the Scion by Toyota brand — a right-sized kei truck for American work and play.

The KEI-01 is an ultra-compact utility truck that brings the functionality, efficiency, and versatility of Japan's legendary kei trucks to American customers — packaged the way only Toyota can deliver. It's the first manifestation of the Scion by Toyota "freedom lab" vision.

Designed to meet low-speed vehicle (LSV) federal safety requirements, it's an affordable, highly capable, remarkably maneuverable little workhorse — built for buyers who need versatility and durability without the size or expense of a traditional pickup.

Full Specifications

  • Engine: Proven 660cc three-cylinder, tuned for low-end torque and fuel efficiency for all-day operation
  • Transmission: Continuously Variable Transmission (CVT)
  • Drivetrain: Standard selectable 4WD with auto mode and push-button lock for mud, snow, and uneven terrain
  • Top speed: 25 mph (complies with FMVSS No. 500 for Low-Speed Vehicles)
  • Drive configuration: Right-hand drive
  • Length / width: Just over 11 feet long, under 5 feet wide
  • Payload: Up to 600 lb in the bed
  • Towing: Structural rigidity for small trailers and equipment
  • Cargo bed: Three-way fold-down for low-height loading/unloading
  • Cabin: Extended cabin with raised roofline, reclining/sliding seats, secure interior storage behind the seats
  • Climate: Enclosed cabin with heat and air conditioning

Small Footprint. Big Capability.

Despite its compact footprint, the KEI-01 delivers real capability, versatility, and operator comfort. The efficient powertrain is built for all-day use, the selectable 4WD tackles rough terrain, and the three-way fold-down bed makes loading effortless. The extended cabin adds comfort and secure storage — a genuinely practical package in a tiny footprint.

Who the KEI-01 Is Built For

  • Small business owners wanting an affordable urban commercial vehicle
  • Farmers and ranchers needing a dependable daily workhorse
  • Municipalities, universities, and corporate campuses seeking efficient fleet vehicles
  • Delivery and service providers operating in dense urban environments
  • Outdoor enthusiasts and hobbyists wanting a versatile utility vehicle

How to Buy One & When

The KEI-01 will be assembled at Toyota Motor Manufacturing Indiana starting in the second half of 2027, backed by a $34 million investment that adds roughly 50 jobs.

As an intentionally low-volume, niche vehicle, it launches through a fleet-focused pilot program sold via a small network of authorized Scion by Toyota dealers (selected from the existing Toyota dealer network). Based on demand and operational factors, Toyota may expand to limited retail sales later. We'll update this page the moment pricing and dealer details are announced.

Aftermarket & Accessories — What We're Hoping For

With the KEI-01 built for work and play, we're genuinely excited to see what the U.S. aftermarket does with it. A right-sized 4WD utility truck is a blank canvas — and the right accessories could make it even more capable and personal.

We'd love to see American manufacturers like Bartact — known for rugged, USA-made seat covers, MOLLE storage, and interior gear for Jeeps and trucks — bring their lineup to this little workhorse. Seat covers built for mud and long workdays, interior MOLLE panels and storage for tools and gear, and durable organization solutions would be a natural fit for how people will actually use the KEI-01. Here's hoping.
